About Non-Contributory Benefits

Non-contributory benefits (SAII - Subsídio de Apoio a Idosos e Inválidos) are social assistance programs for citizens who do not have social security contribution history. These benefits provide financial support to elderly, disabled persons, children, and those in emergency situations.

Please select a benefit type above to view detailed information about eligibility criteria, benefit amounts, and required documents.
